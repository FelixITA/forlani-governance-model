# The ForlAⁿI Governance Model

A Multi-Agent Governance Architecture for Human-Centric Digital Operations.

The ForlAⁿI Governance Model is a proposed architecture for safe AI-assisted digital operations. It separates proposal, critique, execution, and human authority into four roles: Ego, Alter-Ego, Super-Ego, and Deus.

## Problem

Current AI agents often rely on repeated permission prompts to compensate for limited internal governance.

### Figure 1 - Authorization Bottleneck in Current AI Agents

![Current Authorization Workflow](diagrams/current-authorization-loop.png)

A simplified representation of the authorization workflow typically used in current copilots and AI agents. Human intervention is required because proposal, evaluation, and execution preparation are concentrated within the same reasoning process.

## Proposed Solution

### Figure 2 - The ForlAⁿI Governance Model

![ForlAⁿI Governance Model](diagrams/forlani-governance-workflow.png)

A multi-agent governance architecture based on role separation. Ego proposes, Alter-Ego critiques, Super-Ego decides and executes bounded actions, while Deus remains the final human authority for high-risk or ambiguous situations.

## Roles

- Ego proposes actions.
- Alter-Ego independently critiques them.
- Super-Ego executes only bounded, approved operations.
- Deus, the human owner, decides high-risk or ambiguous cases.
